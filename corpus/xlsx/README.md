# Excel corpus (GridCraft and similar)

A set of 57 input files, plus `MANIFEST.tsv`, used to test thumbnail generation (first page of the first visible sheet as PNG) and to compare GridCraft (original and patched) with LibreOffice. Assembled on 2026-10-19.

- Total size: 984 KB (includes `regressao-amostras/`, 176 KB).
- Index: `MANIFEST.tsv` (file, case, category, suportado_gridcraft, expected thumbnail note). It was generated by the agent that assembled the corpus and is the source of truth for the `suportado_gridcraft` column.
- Corpus assembly report (how each case was created, how LibreOffice defines "page 1"): `../../reports/17-corpus-xlsx-CORPUS.md` and `../../reports/09-corpus-xlsx-e-baseline-libreoffice.md`.
- Results: `../../baselines/excel/`.

## Composition

| Category | Files | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Valid (.xlsx) | 42 | 01 to 42 |
| Alternative format (.xls, .ods, .csv and .tsv in UTF-8 and Latin-1) | 6 | 43 to 48 |
| Invalid (0 bytes, truncated, corrupted, plain text, swapped extensions) | 9 | 49 to 57 |

There is no .xlsb, because no local tool writes that format.

## Read with care

- The generator scripts were Python (XlsxWriter 3.2.9 and openpyxl 3.1.5) and are not versioned, by project rule. The generated files are here, and `../../docs/TESTS.md` describes the tools and the cases.
- Two cases depend on external tools: 43 (.xls) and 44 (.ods) were converted from 34 by LibreOffice, and the images in 21 were generated with ImageMagick.
- The 10,000-row spreadsheet (`24-planilha-comprida-10000-linhas.xlsx`) is only 303 KB and is included. Nothing was left out for size.
- Files 50 and 51 derive from 03: 50 is 03 cut in half and 51 is 03 with overwritten bytes. Files 54, 55 and 57 are copies of 02 with a swapped extension, and 56 is a copy of 43.

## regressao-amostras/

16 small spreadsheets (`01_values` to `16_text_misc`) used only by the PDF regression test (`../../baselines/gridcraft-pdf-regression/`), together with the 48 .xlsx spreadsheets of this corpus. They were generated by an unversioned Python script, covering: values, styles, borders, merging, conditional formatting, charts, images, line wrapping, pagination with manual breaks and repeated titles, header and footer, scaling, A3, landscape, fit to page, row and column headings, sparklines, rotated text and overflow.
